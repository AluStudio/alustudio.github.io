# Site glossary — Deutsch (de)

Copied from `_template.md`. The de translator fills it before translating any packet, and the reviewer holds every de value to it.

The Babbby app has its own glossary (`l10n/glossary/de.md` in the babbby-ios repo, `packet.glossary.app`): activity, the eight activity categories, Today's plan, Library, Stats, free trial, Lifetime and every other app term come from there, never from this file. This file holds only the words the website repeats that the app glossary does not define, so every page says them the same way. Never contradict the app glossary.

Fill "Term in language" with the one word or phrase every page will use, as a parent in that market searches for it. Add a row for any other site-only word that repeats across pages.

| en | Term in language | Note |
|----|------------------|------|
| Baby activity app (the product category, as parents search for it) | Baby-Aktivitäten-App | Hyphenated compound as Duden writes it; no "Aktivitäten App". Titles and descriptions may also say "App mit Aktivitäten für Baby und Kind", but the category word stays "Baby-Aktivitäten-App". |
| Activity ideas (daily play ideas for ages 0–6) | Aktivitätsideen | Built on the app's "Aktivität"; never "Spielideen" or "Übungen" for the product's activities. "Daily play ideas" = "tägliche Aktivitätsideen". |
| Ages 0–6 (the age span, as the pages write it) | 0–6 Jahre | En dash, no spaces: "Kinder von 0–6 Jahren", "für 0–6 Jahre". In running FAQ prose "von 0 bis 6 Jahren" is fine. Age bands follow the app ("6–12 Monate"). |
| Parents and guardians | Eltern und Erziehungsberechtigte | Privacy policy and terms (legal precision). Marketing pages and FAQ say just "Eltern". |
| FAQ / frequently asked questions | FAQ | Nav and footer label "FAQ" (common in German); the page title and prose use "Häufig gestellte Fragen". |
| Privacy Policy | Datenschutzerklärung | from app glossary |
| Terms of Use | Nutzungsbedingungen | from app glossary |
| Service Provider (the legal party in the policy and terms) | Dienstanbieter | Defined term in the intro: Alu Studio („Dienstanbieter“); then "der Dienstanbieter" throughout. |
| Application (the legal name for the app in the policy and terms) | App | Defined term „App“ (Babbby-App für iOS); "die App" throughout, also where en says "Application"; never "Applikation" or "Anwendung". |
| Stored on your device (data never leaves the phone) | auf deinem Gerät gespeichert | "ausschließlich auf deinem Gerät gespeichert" for en "stored exclusively on your device"; "wird nie übertragen" for "never transmitted". |
| Home (nav link) | Start | Navigation label for the home page. |
| Language (nav link, language switcher) | Sprache | from app glossary (Settings row). |
| Contact Us | Kontakt | Footer link; the heading and sentence in the policies use "Kontaktiere uns". |
| Daily plan / Today's plan, done. | Tagesplan | from app glossary; "Today's plan, done." is written around "Tagesplan". |
| Milestones (as in "track milestones") | Meilensteine | The word parents search for; the FAQ keeps en's point that tracking shows completed activities, not a developmental assessment. |
| Little one / your child (running text) | dein Baby, dann dein Kind | Per contract; never "dein Kleines" or a gendered pronoun; rephrase instead. |
| Free (price) | kostenlos | "Kostenlos laden" for free download; "Kostenlose Testphase" is the app's trial term, not this. |
| Buy the Developer a Coffee (optional one-time sponsor purchase) | Spendiere dem Entwickler einen Kaffee | Keep the quoted name as written here on every page; the purchase is "freiwilliger Einmalkauf" ("Einmalkauf" as in the app). |
| Download on the App Store (badge, two lines) | Laden im / App Store | Apple's German badge: small line "Laden im", large line "App Store". |
| Lifestyle and Education (App Store categories) | Lifestyle und Bildung | The German App Store category names. |
| Effective Date: March 13, 2026 | Gültig ab: 13. März 2026 | German date format, day first with a full month name. |
| Personal data | personenbezogene Daten | GDPR term; "personal information" = "personenbezogene Daten" too, never "persönliche Informationen". |
| GDPR | DSGVO | Written "DSGVO" with GDPR in parentheses once per page, e.g. „Für Nutzer in der EU/im EWR (DSGVO, englisch GDPR)“; after that just "DSGVO". CCPA stays CCPA. |
| Third-party services | Drittanbieter-Dienste | Hyphenated; "Dienste von Drittanbietern" in running text is the same term. |
| Local database | lokale Datenbank | GRDB/SQLite stays untranslated. |
| Children's profile (name, birth date) | Kinderprofil | Plural "Kinderprofile"; on the site only. The app's own label for the profile is "Baby" and is used when the site names the app screen. |
| Uninstall the app | App löschen | iOS wording ("App löschen"); data is "endgültig gelöscht". |
| Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements | Einstellungen → Datenschutz & Sicherheit → Analyse & Verbesserungen | The path as iOS shows it in German; "Einstellungen" from app glossary. Check the exact iOS labels before changing. |
| Right to erasure / right to be forgotten | Recht auf Löschung („Recht auf Vergessenwerden“) | The DSGVO Art. 17 wording; the other rights follow the regulation: Auskunft, Berichtigung, Einschränkung der Verarbeitung / Widerspruch, Datenübertragbarkeit. |
